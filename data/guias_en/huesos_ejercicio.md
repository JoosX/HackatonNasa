## Why it matters
Without gravity, bones and muscles work less and get weaker. When returning to gravity (or arriving on Mars) the risk of fractures increases.

## What happens in space
According to NASA, weight-bearing bones lose on average 1 to 1.5 % of their density per month on 4 to 6 month missions. With resistance exercise, the loss drops to about 0.3 to 0.5 % per month.

## How to measure on board
Bone density is measured with special equipment before and after the mission. On board, log daily exercise (minutes and type) and any pain in bones or joints.

## Warning signs
Days without resistance exercise, pain in the back, hips or legs, or a blow with pain that does not improve.

## What to do
- Follow the daily resistance and cardiovascular exercise plan.
- Log your exercise minutes in the app.
- After a blow with strong pain or deformity, immobilize the area and alert the crew.

## When to alert
Yellow: 2 or more days in a row without resistance exercise. Red: strong pain after a blow or a suspected fracture.
